## Thesis Context

- **Core question:** Can representations learned by pretrained VLA models be reused for new robotic tasks?
- **Main model:** SmolVLA (and related transformer-based VLAs with FAST tokenization, flow matching, action chunking)
- **Adaptation strategies:** LoRA/RANK adapters, freezing policies, task-specific output heads, low-rank updates in vision/language/action streams
- **Evaluation paradigm:** Similar vs. dissimilar downstream robotic tasks; transferability performance margins
- **Key literature areas:** Transformers, VLA architecture, action tokenization (FAST), flow-based modeling, representation learning, domain adaptation
